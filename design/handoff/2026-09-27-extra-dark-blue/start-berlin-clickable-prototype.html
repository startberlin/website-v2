What is START Berlin?

A place for the next generation of innovators.

We bring ambitious students together with founders, investors, and the wider Berlin startup ecosystem.

Since 2013, START Berlin has connected students from universities across the city through a diverse community, hands-on experiences, and opportunities to learn from people building companies.

Who we
are.

A student-led community at the heart of Berlin's startup scene — turning bold ideas into real ventures since 2013.

Our purpose

Learn by doing.

We believe student-driven innovation can shape the future. START Berlin gives people from across the city's universities a place to connect, learn from practitioners, and explore the startup world together.

From founder conversations and company visits to workshops and community events, members gain practical experience and a supportive network.
